Avocado Sauce

Home

Description

A creamy, fresh blend of avocado, herbs, lemon, and veggies. Perfect as a dip, spread, or healthy topping.

Ingredients

  • A whole avocado
  • 1 tps moringa powder
  • 1 tsp garlic or 2 fresh garlic cloves
  • 1 slice celery
  • 1/4 green onion
  • 1/4 of bell pepper (any color for variety)
  • 1 whole green onion
  • 2tsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • A few sprigs of parsley
  • 2 oz olive oil or avocado oil (adjust to taste)

Steps

  1. Prepare Ingredients

    2. Wash all vegetables and herbs(celery, green onion, parsley, bell peppers).

  2. Peel and Pit

    3. Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a blender or bowl.

  3. Chop

    4. Roughly chop the celery, green onion, bell pepper, garlic (if fresh), and parsley.

  4. Add Everything

    5. Add all ingredients into a blender:

    • Avocado
    • Chopped vegetables
    • Moringa powder
    • Garlic powder or fresh garlic
    • Salt
    • lemon juice
    • Olive oil or avocado oil
  5. Blend

    6. Blend until smooth and creamy. You can add a small amount of oil to adjust consistency if needed.

  6. Taste and Adjust

    7. Taste the sauce and adjust salt, lemon, or garlic to your preference.

  7. Serve or Store

    8. Serve fresh or store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days.
